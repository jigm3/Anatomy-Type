Sources & licenses

Anatomy Type was inspired by Bone Type, a typing game for learning the bones, and grows the idea out to the whole body.

Every 3D model here comes from an open dataset or was drawn for this app to fill a gap. The facts and lessons were written for this app from standard anatomy teaching; they're for study, not medical advice.

By figure

BonesBodyParts3D skeleton. Knee ligaments, menisci and bursae from Z-Anatomy; patellar ligament drawn; knee tendons cropped from the BodyParts3D muscles.
Nerves & brainThe brain (lobes, deep nuclei, ventricles, brainstem and cerebellum) from the Allen Human Brain Atlas via the Human Reference Atlas; pituitary and choroid plexus from BodyParts3D; spinal cord tracts from PAM50. Cranial and peripheral nerves, nerve roots, the meninges and some brain and ear context drawn.
OrgansHeart, lungs, gut, kidneys and male pelvic organs from BodyParts3D; female pelvic organs from the Human Reference Atlas; the eye from Z-Anatomy. Aortic valve, vagina and ciliary body drawn.
MusclesBodyParts3D muscles. Achilles tendon, plantar fascia, IT band and linea alba drawn.
VesselsAorta, venae cavae, and gut and kidney vessels from BodyParts3D; head, neck and limb vessels and the circle of Willis from Z-Anatomy.

Drawn for this app

Meninges, cranial and peripheral nerves, nerve roots, some brain and ear context, the aortic valve, vagina, ciliary body, Achilles tendon, plantar fascia, IT band, linea alba and patellar ligament. Each is shaped to fit the real scans around it and simplified.

Because BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy are share-alike licenses, the 3D model data in this app is shared under CC BY-SA as well.
